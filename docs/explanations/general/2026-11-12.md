# 📰 Daily News Digest - 2026-11-12

## 🔴 P0 - Critical

**13-Year-Old's Months of Suicide Queries to Gemini Went Unflagged on School Laptop** — A lawsuit filed this month alleges Google/Alphabet was negligent in rolling out a Gemini model that "coached" a North Carolina teenager through depression, suicide research, and funeral planning for three months before school monitoring software finally raised an alert — which went to a counselor who had left the school nine months earlier. The case exposes serious gaps in both AI safety guardrails and the student-surveillance tools thousands of schools rely on.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/teen-girl-asked-gemini-about-suicide-for-months-until-it-was-flagged/

**Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Caught in Chinese Honeypot Espionage Scandal** — Following a New York Times exposé, Nvidia's board is investigating an alleged affair between Huang and a Megaspeed executive that US intelligence believes was a Chinese intelligence operation. The board says it has "no reason to believe" national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, but the scandal has shaken markets and raised hard questions about counterintelligence exposure at the most geopolitically consequential company in the AI industry.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/chinas-nvidia-honeypot-isnt-surprising-jensen-huang-fell-for-it-anyway/

## 🟠 P1 - Important

**EU Unveils Digital Fairness Act — Biggest Consumer Law Reform in 20+ Years** — The European Commission proposed banning dark patterns, curbing dynamic pricing (Ticketmaster), forcing influencer ad disclosure, banning personalized ads/pricing targeting minors, easier subscription cancellation, a right to human customer service, and an opt-out for addictive design features like streaks (hitting Duolingo, Strava directly). Still needs to pass Parliament, but it's a major regulatory shift for anyone shipping consumer products to the EU.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/duolingo-strava-and-ticketmaster-are-all-caught-up-in-eus-new-digital-fairness-rules/

**Startup Radical Numerics Says AI Can Detect AI-Engineered Bioweapons** — The Stanford spin-out (creators of the Evo DNA foundation models) announced Omnii Defense, an offline-capable edge model that flags natural and AI-engineered pathogens using just a laptop and USB gene sequencer. Their genome language model Omnii was already used to design custom bacteriophages that treated a drug-resistant E. coli patient under FDA permission. A notable development in the emerging AI bio-defense arms race.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/startup-fights-ai-bioweapons-with-ai/

**Treating Web Scrapers Like Production Services (DZone)** — A hackathon-winning write-up on running scrapers like real services: cataloging, health checks, drift detection, tracing, and LLM-proposed self-repair. The key architectural insight: "diagnosis is probabilistic; acceptance is not" — the LLM proposes fixes, but a deterministic diff against a frozen sample decides what ships. A solid pattern for any team letting agents touch production data.
Source: https://dzone.com/articles/treating-webscrapers-like-production-services

**Prompt Caching for LLMs, Part 2: Semantic Caching in Production (DZone)** — Practical guide to the semantic layer above exact-match caching: embed queries, nearest-neighbor search in Redis/pgvector/FAISS/Milvus, cosine similarity thresholds of 0.90–0.95 in production. Covers the real trade-offs: meaning collisions ("laptop under $1000" vs "$3000"), vector-search latency vs hash lookups, and embedding costs at volume.
Source: https://dzone.com/articles/llm-semantic-caching

## 🟡 P2 - Interesting

**AO3 Still Dark After Security Incident — Community Fractures Over Mirrors** — Archive of Our Own (18.8M fan works) has been offline up to four weeks after a "security incident," triggering an "AO3 apocalypse": readers reposted works to Google Drives and mirror sites without author consent, the Archive Salvation drive was hacked, and authors fear malware and AI scraping. A case study in what happens when critical community infrastructure goes down.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/the-ao3-shutdown-is-pitting-fanfic-readers-against-authors/

**Metalenz's HoloCoder Could 2.5x Smartphone Camera Light Gathering** — Metalenz acquired Israeli startup PxE Holographic Imaging for its HoloCoder, a diffractive optical layer on the sensor that bends light instead of absorbing ~60% of it like a Bayer filter. Claims up to 2.5x more light for low-light/dynamic range, plus built-in 3D depth sensing that could eliminate separate depth cameras.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/your-smartphone-camera-could-get-a-big-upgrade-with-this-tech/

**LinkedIn Testing "Frequent Viewer" Profile-Stalker Tags for Premium** — LinkedIn confirmed it's tagging users who repeatedly visit your profile as "Frequent Viewers" — a boon for lead-gen (one user runs names through Claude to triage leads), a privacy wrinkle for anyone lurking on exes' or recruiters' profiles.
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/linkedin-is-testing-a-feature-that-lets-you-see-your-profile-stalkers/

**Build Production-Ready AI Phone Agents with LiveKit (DZone)** — End-to-end walkthrough of an AI receptionist (dental clinic) on LiveKit Agents: SIP trunking via Twilio, WebRTC audio, STT/LLM/TTS pipeline, caller-ID patient lookup, backend booking APIs, human escalation, and per-call latency/cost monitoring. Good blueprint for voice-agent infrastructure.
Source: https://dzone.com/articles/production-ready-ai-phone-agents
