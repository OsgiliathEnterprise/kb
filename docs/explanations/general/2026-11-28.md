### ESA's Hera returns the first images of the DART impact site — a "chaotic scene"
Four years after NASA's DART probe deliberately slammed into asteroid Dimorphos (releasing ~10 billion joules at 6.14 km/s and shortening its orbit around Didymos by 33 minutes — 25x the conservative expectation), ESA's Hera mission has sent back the first close-up images of the crater field. DART destroyed itself on impact, so until now only distant telescopes and a brief CubeSat impact-flash had seen the aftermath; Hera (with CubeSats Juventas and Milani) is filling in "the missing pieces of the puzzle" about what an kinetic impactor actually does to a rubble-pile asteroid. Directly relevant to planetary-defense credibility.
Source: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/europes-hera-mission-returns-to-the-chaotic-scene-of-a-cosmic-smashup/)

### Zero-Downtime Migrations: the Expand/Contract discipline
A war-story-driven, genuinely substantive guide: an 11-second staging `ALTER TABLE` became a 38-minute prod lock that took down checkout. Includes an excellent engine-by-engine lock cheat sheet (MySQL 5.7 vs 8.0 instant DDL vs Postgres 14+: which operations rebuild, lock, or queue behind a stray analytics query) and the three-phase Expand → Migrate → Contract pattern from Martin Fowler's talk: deploy schema additively, backfill in batches, only then contract away the old column. The rule to internalize: for every migration you ship, you must be able to answer "what's the lock mode, how long, and what's queuing behind it."
Source: [DEV.to](https://dev.to/mango7777/zero-downtime-migrations-the-expandcontract-discipline-that-keeps-prod-alive-29ec)

### Next.js hydration failures: 6 causes — and the "fix" that leaks a secret
Solid breakdown of "Hydration failed because the initial UI does not match": browser-only values in render, `Math.random()`/`new Date()` in output, invalid HTML nesting, extension-injected nodes, divergent server/client data, and locale/timezone formatting. Fixes ranked (read React's diff, move browser reads to `useEffect`, `dynamic(..., {ssr:false})`, don't blanket-suppress). The security takeaway is the best part: hard-coding a key into the component so server and client match ships the secret to every visitor — only `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` reaches the browser, and it must never hold a secret.
Source: [DEV.to](https://dev.to/proofforge_live/nextjs-hydration-failed-because-the-initial-ui-does-not-match-6-causes-the-fix-and-the-one-11g1)

### An Italian agency published open data on how often AI assistants choose their brand
Wespo's "Agent Behavior Lab" built Eligo, which repeatedly asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Grok purchase-intent questions about Italian performance-marketing agencies and measures Choice Share (actively recommended: 7% → 11% between two scans), Presence (mentioned: 24% → 28%), and which domains get cited. Two findings generalize: assistants lean heavily on third-party marketplaces/review sites rather than your own site, and run-to-run variance is so high that single-shot "AI visibility" checks are noise. Aggregated results published monthly on GitHub — one of the first open measurement baselines for LLM-driven brand discovery.
Source: [DEV.to](https://dev.to/wespoagency/how-often-do-ai-assistants-choose-your-brand-we-measured-it-open-data-mo4)

### 5 PHP memory mistakes that kill scripts
Raising `memory_limit` just buys time. The five: whole-table `get()`/`all()` (use `chunk()`), editing the wrong `php.ini` (CLI and PHP-FPM read different files — `php --ini`), `file_get_contents()` on huge files (stream with `fgets`), returning big arrays instead of `Generator` yields, and unbounded caching inside long-running workers. Solid checklist even outside PHP — all five are generic memory-hygiene patterns.
Source: [DEV.to](https://dev.to/rajadevsolutions/5-php-memory-mistakes-that-kill-your-script-and-the-fix-for-each-52jp)

### "The Zero-Upload Web": client-side architecture as a privacy play

Promotional undertone (lists the author's tool suite) but the architecture comparison — server pipeline vs. local `FileReader` → WASM worker → `createObjectURL` pipeline — is accurate and worth knowing for anyone building document tools.
Source: [DEV.to](https://dev.to/prajwa__daca5c75e95657/-the-zero-upload-web-how-client-side-browser-architecture-protects-sensitive-documents-media-1d8h)
